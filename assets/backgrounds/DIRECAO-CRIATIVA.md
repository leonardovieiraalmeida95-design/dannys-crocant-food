Warm directional studio light, tactile satin matte surfaces, rich shadows, quiet sophistication. No text, no typography, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no recognizable chicken pieces, no plates, no cutlery, no neon, no bright yellow background. This is a decorative backdrop, low contrast and atmospheric, not a standalone poster.
